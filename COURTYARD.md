# THE COURTYARD -- a small, organised level that puts the buildings to work

His ask (after m1264), with the reference `images/korajin/ref/timber_lane_watchtower.jpg` (a stone lane between tall dark-timber
halls hung with lanterns, running to a two-roofed watchtower over the far gate):

> *"Building a small courtyard would let us put some of these to action. It needs a good layout and plan: watch guards in the towers,
> watch guards on the ground, grass, a stony path, some centrepieces, a pond, some fairy dust, nice sunlight, some creatures -- but
> keeping it SMALL and ORGANISED. The layout is super important. We want a lot of verticality and height, large scale, and things
> connecting."*

## The rules this plan keeps

- **Small:** the whole court is inside **56 x 64 m**. You can see every part of it from the watchtower top. Nothing is filler.
- **One axis, one loop.**
  - **The axis:** the stone path runs straight from the SOUTH GATE to the WATCHTOWER, so the court reads the moment you walk in.
  - **The loop:** a raised wall-walk circles the whole court, so the court can be run without ever touching the ground.
- **Four height bands, all connected:**
  - **L0** the ground (path, grass, pond)
  - **L1 4 m** (the hall balconies, the wall-walk)
  - **L2 9 m** (the hall roofs, the rope bridges)
  - **L3 15 m+** (the watchtower top, the pagoda's upper storeys)

  Every band has at least two ways up and one way down that is FAST (a zipline or a jump into the pond).
- **Every building is one we already build or have queued.** The court is a LAYOUT of them, not a new pile of geometry:
  - the Great Red Pagoda (X1)
  - the Timber Lane Halls (new, after the reference)
  - the Watchtower Gate (new, the reference's tower)
  - the dressing kit (R9)
  - the guards we already have (archers, the horde samurai, sentry guns)

## The routes (it must play, not just look)

1. **The front door:** gate, the lane between the halls, the moon bridge, the path to the watchtower, up the tower. The obvious
   route, and the guarded one.
2. **The high road:**
   - from the gate's lintel, along the wall-walk west
   - up the W hall's ladder to its roof (L2)
   - across the rope bridge to the E hall
   - the second bridge to the bell house
   - the wall-walk north to the watchtower's upper deck

   No ground at all.
3. **The climber:**
   - the pagoda from the inside (ladders through the hatches) to storey 4
   - the ZIPLINE down over the court into the pond
   - or the jump from the pagoda's top balcony to the watchtower's lower roof
4. **The fast exit:** from the watchtower top, the zipline to the S gate (the whole court below you).

## The guards (a layout of them, not a crowd)

- **Towers:**
  - archers on the watchtower (2), the pagoda (1) and the hall balconies (2)
  - a sentry gun in the watchtower lookout
  - they hold their posts and shoot

  m1255's look-up lens helps you see them.
- **Ground:**
  - 2 samurai at the gate
  - 1 at the bell house
  - a patrol of 2 that walks the loop (path, pond, halls)

  They notice you on sight. The bell calls them all.
- **Killable, every one.** The court is cleared when the watchtower's gun and its archers are down. Clearing it opens the watchtower gate
  (the way on, later: the Four Fires / the chamber).

## The life

- **Creatures:**
  - koi in the pond (scatter when you land)
  - birds on the roofs (lift off when you run along them)
  - a fox that trots the grass and hides under the halls
- **Light:**
  - the forge's sun low and warm, raking down the lane like the reference
  - sun shafts through the watchtower
  - lanterns everywhere (they glow at dusk)
  - fairy dust: the pooled motes (`SPK`) drifting over the pond and the grass
- **Ground:**
  - the stone path as cut flags
  - grass patches (the dressing kit's tufts, swaying)
  - moss at the wall feet
  - puddles that catch the lantern light

## How it is built (so it stays organised)

- **One entry: SPAWN > BUILDINGS > Courtyard (`COURT`).** It builds the court's own pieces (walls, wall-walk, gate, halls, pond, path,
  watchtower, bell house, bridges) and places the existing builders INSIDE it at court-relative spots. Each existing builder takes an
  `at` override, so the Great Red Pagoda is the same code at a different place.
- **Its own origin:** somewhere clear in the forge (decided at pass 1, measured against every `at` in `BLDG.list`).
- **Passes** (one build each, the loop's rules):
  1. the walls and the wall-walk, the gate, the lane and path, the two Timber Lane Halls (shells, balconies, roofs), the pond with
     the moon bridge
  2. the Watchtower Gate (the reference's tower: two roofs, two decks, the inner gate)
  3. the bell house, the rope bridges, the corner ladders: the high road complete
  4. the pagoda placed in the NW, its zipline to the pond; the watchtower's zipline out; the centrepiece tree
  5. the guards: posted archers and the sentry gun, the gate pair, the patrol loop, the bell's alarm
  6. the life: koi, birds, the fox; fairy dust and sun shafts; lanterns
  7. the ground: flags, grass, moss, puddles, the dressing kit throughout
  8. the clear condition and the watchtower gate opening; the set piece
  9. playtest-driven fixes from his notes (sight-lines, heights, gaps)
  10. polish against the reference: the lane's look, the light, the scale

It is queued as **C1** in BUILDINGS.md, after X1, as the first place the X buildings get used together.
