> *"One of our sandboxes should be projectile weapons -- all we have is the rocket launcher. A bow and arrow definitely, probably a boomerang,

> retro AND futuristic: weird robots mixing with ancient stuff. Instead of a grappling hook, boost jet boots -- not flying, a little hover boot.
> He already has really incredible ninja skills (the wall run, the planks, the jumping) which we still need to refine."*

## THE PRINCIPLE: keep Zelda's ROLES, give each one Korajin's FORM
Zelda's tools last because each one is a ROLE the level design needs: reach far, hit at range, pull, cross a gap, trigger a switch you can't
touch. The role is not the copy -- the form is. Korajin's form is **Jin tech: ancient Japanese craft run on Jinra** (a samurai-era object with
a mint glow and a little machine inside), which is the retro-futurism he wants: old robots and old stone, not chrome.

| Zelda's role | the obvious copy | **Korajin's form (proposal)** | what makes it ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| hit at range, puzzles | bow | **the Jin bow** (yumi) | arrows that STICK and become anchors: a **line arrow** strings a zip line between where you stand and where it hit (the zip lines already exist) |
| returning throw, hit switches | boomerang | **the Jin disc** (a spinning shuriken-ring) | it comes back, and while it is out the RIGHT THUMB steers it; it ricochets off stone, cuts ropes, carries fire from a brazier |
| pull yourself / pull things | grappling hook | **the kusarigama** (chain and sickle) | period-right: hook a post and swing, yank a shield out of an Osari's hands, pull a ledge down, reel a small enemy in |
| cross gaps, float | hover boots / glider | **the Jin geta** (wooden sandals with Jinra jets) | not flight: a short hover (~1 s), a mid-air dash, a slow fall. It EXTENDS his ninja moves (a wall run that ends in a hover-step to the next wall) rather than replacing them |
| blow things up | bombs | **paper lantern bombs** | a lit lantern you set down or throw; it floats a little on its own heat (a bomb that drifts up to a high switch) |
| big gun | hookshot / cannon | **the tanegashima** (the Japanese matchlock) firing Jinra | the rocket launcher re-dressed as an ancient hand cannon: retro iron outside, mint machinery inside |
| see the hidden | lens of truth | **the spirit lantern** | shows Jinra trails, hidden paths, the stolen ones inside the statues |
| glide | paraglider / Deku leaf | **the war kite** | a samurai kite he hangs from; rides updrafts off braziers |

The sword-and-ninja kit stays the CORE. Every tool is a reason to use the core differently (a line arrow makes a zip line where there was none;
the geta make a wall run reach further), never a replacement for it. The ARSENAL ladders (ARSENAL.md) are these tools' upgrade tiers.

## THE SANDBOX
**A white range** (the forge): targets at several heights and distances (VERTICALITY rule), posts and wood beams to stick into, a gap too wide
to jump, switches out of reach, a rope to cut, an Osari bowman and a shield-bearer to test on. Each tool is one piece, built and signed off on
its own before the next.

## THE QUICK-DRAW RING (his, Oct 29 2026 -- the UI refactor after the bow)
> *"A really quick, easy way to always go between projectile and melee weapon -- above the right stick. The left thumb is always your motion, so
> keep it free. Two meaningfully sized buttons, the first row of a ring cut in half: projectile on one, melee on the other, so you can be
> fighting, fighting, tap the other, shoot shoot shoot, back to fighting -- you can only block with the melee weapon out. A third above them
> for unarmed (ninja stuff). The sub-menu for whatever weapon is equipped moves to the LEFT: the bow's arrows (regular, fire, exploding)
> picked there."*
- **Above the right pad:** a ring split into two big halves -- MELEE (the katana) and PROJECTILE (the bow / the launcher) -- and a third segment
  above them for UNARMED. One tap swaps; the swap is fast enough to block, shoot, block.
- **Above the left pad:** the equipped weapon's SUB-MENU (the arrow types for the bow; the katana's or the launcher's options later). It moves
  from the right side to the left, the opposite of today.
- **Built at m1724** (he asked for it before the bow's sign-off). TOOLS > QUICK DRAW turns it off.
