# KORAJIN: brief for enemy concept art

Paste this into an image generator, then ask for prompts and images. The section at the end covers what to ask for.

## The game
KORAJIN is a stylized 3D action-adventure for mobile. It's a hand-to-hand and sword fighting game with parkour and flying. The look is chunky, readable, painterly 3D in the spirit of modern stylized games: bold silhouettes, big simple shapes and rich colour. The world is a fantasy mix of feudal Japan, a dusty Western frontier, and ancient alien technology so old it has become tradition.

## The story
- **The Korajin were once a magical sky people.** Their floating cities were held up by JINRA, a luminous, semi-living essence: a translucent mint-green liquid with glowing motes. It can be liquid, vapour or crystal, and it powers life, machines, weapons and flight.
- **The Jinra was stolen.** The sky world fell to the ground and became rusty, dusty and broken. The Korajin forgot they were ever magical and live as ordinary frontier folk.
- **The hero is Grumbo,** a tough, stubborn, oddly kind warrior. He fights to win the Jinra back, wake his people, and restore the floating world.

## The enemy: who they are
- **They are ancient CONSTRUCTS,** not living soldiers: warriors of carved stone and fired terracotta, like the Sphinx and the buried terracotta army.
- **They are jointed like old machines.** Stolen Jinra holds them together and glows mint-green inside them, through a core in the chest, the eyes, and the cracks and seams at the joints. The brighter the glow, the stronger the construct.
- **When they are broken,** chunks of stone and clay fall away and the green essence seeps out and rises into the air.
- **The master behind them is a mystery:** a colossal construct, or a hidden power.

## The enemy: their architecture (what I want concepts for)
- **Base style:** classical feudal Japanese fortress and temple architecture, made sinister and ancient:
  - castle keeps on battered stone bases
  - layered curved roofs
  - fortified gates
  - pagodas, watchtowers, barracks, walls and shrines
- **Built of:** carved stone, fired terracotta, black lacquered timber and dark bronze. It is heavy, imposing, symmetrical and severe. Add carved guardian faces and statues in the construct style: Sphinx-like stone beasts and terracotta soldiers.
- **Stolen Jinra burns in their structures:**
  - **TORCH TOWERS:** giant braziers on tall towers burning with mint-green Jinra fire. These are the key landmark of every enemy base.
  - glowing green core chambers, pipes and vats draining the essence
  - green light leaking from cracks, windows and lanterns
- **Palette: the OPPOSITE of the heroes.** The heroes use warm cream sandstone, red cloth with cream diamond patterns, dark timber, brass and bronze, and round domes and striped tent cones. The enemy should feel cold, hard and angular. I'm still choosing between:
  - **(A)** black lacquer with a GOLD or yellow emblem
  - **(B)** black with acid-green accents
  - **(C)** bone-white stone with black and gold
- **Their emblem:** a sharp, angular crest, distinct from the heroes' rounded square-spiral and nested-diamond motifs. Shown on banners, gates and roof crests.
- **Readable at a glance from a game camera:** strong silhouettes, clear tiers and levels (they are climbed in the game), visible weak points and breakable parts (they get torn down).

## Avoid
- The heroes' look: domes, striped tent cones, red-and-cream diamond banners, ship wheels.
- Realistic or photographic rendering, and modern or sci-fi chrome.
- Any real people or real-world symbols and flags.

## What to ask for
1. Five image prompts, one for each enemy building:
   - a TORCH TOWER with a green Jinra brazier
   - a fortress KEEP
   - a fortified GATE
   - a BARRACKS where constructs stand dormant
   - a SHRINE where Jinra is drained
2. One concept-art sheet of the enemy architecture kit: roofs, gates, walls, braziers, banners and statues, on one sheet, like a style guide.
3. One sheet each for the three palette options (A, B, C) on the same torch tower, so I can pick.
4. One sheet of the constructs themselves: a terracotta foot soldier, a stone Sphinx-like beast, an archer and a giant, each with the green glow inside.
