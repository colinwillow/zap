# THE ARSENAL -- what blocks what, and how gear climbs (brainstorm, Oct 28 2026; his to cut)

> *"Right now your guy can block arrows with his sword and that's fun, but it would be crazy for him to block lasers, or cannon rock, fire,
> rockets. Maybe you get a reflective shield that can deflect lasers, or a rock or steel shield that blocks explosions. Like Zelda: a wooden
> sword, a metal sword, a fire sword. Arrows: wooden, hardened, fire, explosive -- then a rocket launcher, a cannon. Upgrade the dragon.
> Wardrobe, like Link's fire and water outfits. We'll probably need a diagram to map what does what. You go into a new level used to
> blocking arrows, some guy shoots at you, you try to block it and it just FRIES you -- what the hell. Maybe people try the level without
> upgrading and it's possible, but really, really hard. Basically you go do the mission that upgrades your shield; the village people give
> you his stuff."*

The visual version of this page (the chart) is an Artifact: **Korajin Arsenal** (https://claude.ai/artifact/2vH5a2xE359ymbQGdKRejh).

## THE RULE: every new THREAT arrives before its ANSWER
1. **The threat is introduced in a new region, unannounced.** The first eye-laser is a WARNING: telegraphed (a red sight line, a charge-up
   whine), it scorches and knocks you back but does not kill. You try your parry. It fries you. *What the hell* is the lesson.
2. **The answer is in the village, from a person you can help.** The smith, once awakened (KORAJIN.md, THE RESTORATION), forges the shield
   that beats it, for a mission or an artifact. **The awakened townspeople ARE the upgrade shops**: the Jin smith (blades, shields), the Jin
   fletcher (arrows), the Jin weaver (outfits), the stable master (the dragon).
3. **A SOFT GATE, never a wall.** Without the upgrade the level can still be beaten (dodge, roll, cover, timing), but it is brutal. Speed-runners
   will love it; everyone else goes and gets the shield. A hard gate exists only where the item is a VERB the route needs (the grapple, the glider).
4. **The answer is then used against the threat's owner.** The mirror shield sends the laser back; the boss of that temple is beaten with it
   (ZELDA_STRUCTURE.md, part 2: the item is taught, tested, twisted, and the boss is the exam).

## WHAT BLOCKS WHAT
`✓` blocks · `⟲` reflects it back · `½` blunts (some damage, knockback) · `✗` fails (you take it all) · `🔥` the shield burns up

| | blade | arrow | rock | fire | laser | explosion | curse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **katana parry** (start) | ⟲ | ⟲ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| **wooden shield** | ✓ | ✓ | ½ | 🔥 | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| **iron shield** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ (heats, fails) | ½ | ✗ |
| **mirror shield** (polished bronze) | ✓ | ✓ | ½ | ✓ | ⟲ | ½ | ⟲ (late) |
| **stone / Jin ward** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **roll / dodge** | any, if timed | | | | | | |

The wooden shield BURNING is Ocarina of Time's Deku Shield, and it is the best kind of lesson: it fails in a way you can see.

## THE TRACKS (each climbs in tiers; acts from ZELDA_STRUCTURE.md)
| track | prologue | act I (ground) | act II (below) | act III (sky) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **blade** | the katana | steel katana (more damage, the full-charge halving) | Jin-forged katana (cuts stone, breaks guards) | elemental edges (fire / frost / storm, by Jinra infusion) |
| **shield** | -- (parry only) | wooden -> iron | mirror (reflects lasers; light puzzles in the temples) | Jin ward (blocks everything; the curse) |
| **bow** | -- | wooden arrows -> hardened (pierce shields) | fire arrows (burn ivy, light braziers, melt ice) | blast arrows; Jinra arrows (home in, crack stone) |
| **heavy** | -- | -- | hand cannon / the rocket (blows walls) | the Jin cannon |
| **dragon** | -- | -- | tamed: flight + fireball | saddle (dive-bomb), armour (laser-proof), the storm breath |
| **wardrobe** | the red cloth | **ember coat** (heat zones, fire floors) | **tide gear** (breathe and walk underwater) | **sky cloak** (glide, wind); **stone ward** (curse) |
| **Jin powers** (Jinra) | the charge strikes | the tornado, the zoom | the deflect wave | the green fire, the time slow |

Each tier is a reason to go back to an old place: fire arrows burn the ivy you walked past in act I; the tide gear opens the flooded room in the
prologue temple (the Metroid half of Zelda).
