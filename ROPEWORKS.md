# ROPEWORKS -- the bridges, the drawbridge wheel, the modular towers (his, Oct 29 2026)

> *"I want to spend a bunch of time making the rope bridge really, really interesting -- dynamics, weight, velocity, sway -- and looking really
> stylized, very Japanese: samurai, ninja, alien. A bunch of passes. Don't lose track of that."*

## 1. THE ROPE BRIDGE -- passes (m1800 is pass 0: it hangs, it dips under him, it is a floor)
Each pass is one build, measured headless, judged on his phone. In order:
- **Dynamics A -- weight and bounce:** a real dip under him (today ~13 cm), the bridge RECOILS when he jumps off a plank (it springs up under
  the take-off), a LANDING on it slams it down and it bounces back, running makes it ripple ahead and behind him.
- **Dynamics B -- sway and balance:** sideways momentum swings the whole span; walking near an edge tilts it; HE feels it: his balance pose
  leans against the tilt (the balance beam's lean, `BEAM`), and a big swing can throw him to grab the hand rope.
- **Dynamics C -- the hand ropes are real:** he cannot walk off the side unless he jumps it; he can grab the hand rope and shimmy, hang under
  the deck, swing up.
- **Look A -- Japanese:** lashed bamboo or dark-lacquer planks with rope bindings at every plank, thick twisted rope with a fibre texture,
  shimenawa-style knots and paper SHIDE on the end posts, red-lacquer post caps, small hanging lanterns that swing with the span.
- **Look B -- Korajin (alien samurai):** Jinra-lit knots that pulse when weight is on them, a faint turquoise glow along the ropes, worn and
  weathered planks, moss.
- **Look C -- life:** creak and rope-strain sounds by load, dust off the planks when he lands, a plank that SNAPS under a heavy landing (repairable,
  or a hazard), birds that take off from the hand rope.
- **Variants:** long and steep (one end high), a broken bridge with gaps to jump, a bridge that burns (fire arrows cut it), a bridge enemies cross
  too (their weight moves it under him).

## 2. THE DRAWBRIDGE AND ITS WHEEL (the CAPSTAN)
- A capstan: a pole out of the ground with a ship's wheel mounted FLAT on it (pegs sticking out). A rope (and gears) runs from it to a drawbridge.
- **The input:** walk up to it, HOLD the right stick to take hold, then circle the LEFT stick round -- each turn winds the rope; the wheel spins,
  the rope shortens, the bridge rises (or lowers). Let go: a ratchet holds it (or it slowly unwinds, a choice per wheel).
- **Remote wheels (later):** a wheel far from what it moves, with nothing visibly joining them -- in a temple, up a high ledge, behind a boss, in
  a cage. Wound to a set point it LOCKS and finishes by itself; a Zelda-style jingle plays, the camera cuts away to show the giant bridge (or two
  bridges) swinging into place, the two places now joined, and back (`CINEMA.md`'s reel player is the tool for that cut).
- Spend the time to make it look and feel great: the ratchet click per peg, the rope creaking taut, the bridge's chains, the dust as it lands.

## 3. MODULAR TOWERS
- A tower kit: a shaft and a HEAD, in 2-4 styles. The head has PORTS on its sides: 1 (an end), 2 (a corner or a straight), 3 (a junction), 4 (a hub).
- Every port takes a CONNECTOR: a rope bridge, a solid sky bridge, a balcony, a ledge, a side turret. Bridges join tower to tower; the web is
  the level.
- **Multi-storey:** a second ring of ports higher up; ladders (and rope ladders) between storeys; a three-dimensional maze of bridges.
- **Life:** destructible pieces like the kit buildings, fire-pit lanterns, archers firing down from the heads, enemies crossing the bridges.
- Built as a kit the levels assemble from (like the building kit), not one-offs.
